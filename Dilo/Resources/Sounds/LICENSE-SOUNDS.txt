MarimbaBegin.wav / MarimbaEnd.wav / MarimbaPaste.wav
  Synthesized in-repo by scripts/generar-sonidos-marimba.py
  (additive marimba bar: 1x/3.9x/9.2x partials, 3 ms attack, 300 ms tail,
  48 kHz mono, peak -14 dBFS). Original Dilo assets — no external license,
  and no bytes from anyone else's recording.
  The Tauri build's marimba WAVs were NOT ported: they reached that tree
  through cjpais/handy#214 with no declared provenance, so they are not
  shippable. These were written from scratch to fill the same role.

(header resampling / repetition) and carry the same license as the
